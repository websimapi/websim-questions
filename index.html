❓ How to Ask a Good WebSim Question

Keep questions clear, specific, and WebSim-related.

Include: what you tried, what happened, and a websim.ai link or screenshot.

✅ Good Examples

  • “My WebSim page isn’t loading after I publish: https://websim.ai/@username/myapp. Any ideas?”
  • “When I try to save my project at https://websim.ai/@username/myapp , I get a ‘500 server error’. Does anyone know how to fix this?”
  • “After publishing my project (https://websim.ai/@me/myapp), the site loads fine for me but friends just see a blank page. I checked console logs and see: Uncaught ReferenceError: startGame is not defined. Any idea why this only happens on the published version?”

❌ Not Good Examples

  • “It’s broken. Help.”
  • “What’s the best GPU for gaming?”
  • “How do I fix my WiFi?”

👉 Stay on-topic so the community can help you faster!
